## Records Desk

**Headline:**
Everything we build starts with a public records request.

**Subhead:**
This is every request we have filed, when we filed it, what came back, and the document itself. If a request was denied, you will see why.

**Request log (dynamic table):**
Columns: Date filed · Agency · What we asked for · Status · Response time · Documents
Status values: Filed · Partial · Fulfilled · Denied

**Section: File your own**
Heading: You can do this too.
Body: Ohio's Public Records Act gives every person the right to request public records from any public office. You do not have to say who you are or why you want them. Here is how it works in Toledo.
Steps:
1. Figure out which office holds the record. (TPS, the City of Toledo, and Lucas County each have their own.)
2. Write one clear sentence describing what you want. Be specific about dates.
3. Send it by email to the records officer. We list the addresses below.
4. Public offices must respond in a reasonable time. If they deny, they must tell you why in writing.
5. If you get stuck, write to us. We will help.
Records officer addresses: [TPS] · [City of Toledo] · [Lucas County]

## About

**Headline:**
We read what nobody has time to read.

**Body:**
School budgets, salary schedules, board votes, levies, and city finances are all public. But they arrive as hundreds of pages nobody has time for. The Mona K Project reads them and turns them into a page a teacher, a parent, or a taxpayer can understand in four minutes, with every number linked to the document it came from.

We do not take positions, endorse candidates, or recommend how to vote. We show what the records say and let Toledo decide.

**Section: Principles**
- Every number links to its source. If we cannot source it, we do not publish it.
- Plain language. If it takes more than four minutes, we rewrite it.
- No positions. We explain the records; Toledo decides.
- Reviewed before release. Nothing goes out without the advisory council reading it first.
- Free, always. No paywalls, no memberships, no premium reports.
