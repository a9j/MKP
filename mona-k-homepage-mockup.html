How a 400-page PDF becomes a four-minute read.

We file the request

Under Ohio's Public Records Act. Every request is logged publicly.

We read every page

Budgets, schedules, minutes, forecasts. All of it.

We explain it plainly

With every figure linked to the page it came from.

The council reviews it

Teachers, parents, and accountants read it before anyone else.

We publish and brief

Free to everyone. Offered as a briefing to the union, the board, and the press.
